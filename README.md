# Sales Performance & Variance Analysis Report

An interactive Power BI report designed to analyze monthly sales performance,
manufacturing cost, actual selling price, margin, and price variance.

The report compares actual selling prices against manufacturing costs and
market reference prices to understand pricing differences and overall sales
performance.

## About the Report

This report was created to provide a detailed view of sales performance
through a combination of monthly analysis, margin analysis, and market price
comparison.

The analysis focuses on understanding how actual selling prices differ from
the manufacturing cost and how they compare with other market sellers.

The report uses a single-page layout to bring the key sales and pricing
insights together in one view.

## Key Analysis

- Monthly sales performance
- Manufacturing cost analysis
- Actual selling price analysis
- Average margin
- Actual sales vs manufacturing cost
- Actual sales vs market prices
- Positive and negative price variance
- Monthly market price comparison
- Detailed sales and variance analysis

## Business Logic

The report focuses on two main comparisons.

**Actual Sales vs Manufacturing Cost**

Shows the difference between the actual selling price and manufacturing
cost to understand the margin generated.

**Actual Sales vs Market**

Compares the actual selling price against market reference prices to
identify whether the selling price is higher or lower than other sellers.

The dashboard uses positive and negative variance indicators to make these
differences easier to understand.

## Report Structure

The dashboard brings together:

**Sales Performance → Manufacturing Cost → Actual Sales → Margin → Market Comparison → Price Variance**

This provides a consolidated view of sales and pricing performance in a
single report.

## Data Privacy

The original report concept was developed using business data from a
real-world project.

For this portfolio version, the original product information, company
names, and business identifiers have been anonymized and replaced with
fictional names.

The PBIX file included in this repository contains the portfolio version
of the report and does not use the original client identifiers.
